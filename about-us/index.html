OUR MISSION

Knowledge should be useful, not intimidating.

KnowMint is a part of ShivMarg.com. It is a ShivMarg.com publication created to make useful knowledge about finance, AI, science and technology easier to understand.

What we cover

We publish educational articles across finance, artificial intelligence, science, technology and related areas. Some pieces are practical guides; others are explainers designed to provide context around ideas and developments.

How we approach content

We aim for clear language, useful structure, transparent sourcing and a distinction between established facts, analysis and opinion. Topics that can change quickly should be checked against current primary or authoritative sources before publication or action.

Important: KnowMint is an educational publication. Financial, medical, legal and other specialized topics are not a substitute for advice from a qualified professional.

Our long-term goal

Build a trusted library that remains useful months and years after an article is published, while updating important pages when facts, standards or technology change.
