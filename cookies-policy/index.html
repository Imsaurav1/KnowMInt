1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by a website in a browser. Similar technologies can remember settings or help a service understand how a page is used.

2. How KnowMint may use them

Strictly necessary cookies may support security or basic site functionality. Google Analytics cookies help measure visits and page activity. Google Ads tags help measure advertising conversions. Google AdSense may use cookies to support advertising. These services may process information under Google's policies and settings.

3. Your choices

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking necessary cookies may affect how the website works. Where consent is legally required, optional cookies should remain disabled until you make a choice through the site's consent controls.

4. Third-party cookies

Third-party services may place cookies under their own names and policies. KnowMint does not control their technology, retention or use of information. Review the provider's privacy information before enabling optional services.

This policy may be updated when the website adds or removes services. The date above shows the latest revision.
