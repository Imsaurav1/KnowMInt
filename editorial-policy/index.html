Editorial Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

KnowMint aims to publish useful, understandable and responsible articles. We separate established facts from analysis, identify uncertainty and correct important errors.

1. Scope and standards

We cover finance, AI, science, technology and related subjects. Articles should have a clear purpose, readable structure and claims that can be checked against reliable sources.

2. Research and sources

Writers should prefer primary sources, official documentation, reputable research and established reporting. Sources are reviewed for relevance and date, especially when a topic changes quickly.

3. Accuracy and updates

We may revise articles to fix factual errors, clarify wording, improve links or reflect important changes. Material corrections should be made promptly and should not quietly change the meaning of a claim.

4. Independence and conflicts

Editorial decisions should not be controlled by advertisers, sponsors or commercial partners. Financial relationships, affiliate links, gifts or other relevant conflicts will be disclosed when they could affect how readers evaluate the content.

5. AI-assisted work

AI tools may assist with brainstorming, structure or editing, but published claims remain subject to human review. We do not treat generated text as a source, and we check important facts before publication.

6. Corrections and feedback

Readers can report a possible error, missing context or broken source through the Contact page. Include the article title, the specific passage and a reliable source where possible.

7. Opinion and sponsored content

Analysis and opinion should be distinguishable from reporting. Sponsored, promotional or affiliate content should be labelled clearly and should not be presented as independent editorial work.
