Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

KnowMint respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit this website, why it may be used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we collect

When you browse KnowMint, the website may receive limited technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, requested pages and the date and time of a visit. This information is normally supplied by your browser or hosting provider and is used to deliver and protect the website.

If you contact KnowMint, we may receive the name, email address and message you choose to provide. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information through a general enquiry.

2. How we use information

Information may be used to operate, maintain, secure and improve the website; respond to enquiries and correction requests; investigate abuse; and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

3. Cookies and third parties

KnowMint uses Google Analytics and Google Ads tags to measure page visits and advertising conversions. Google AdSense may also be used to serve advertising. These services may collect information through cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies. See the Cookies Policy for more information. Third-party websites have their own privacy practices.

4. Retention and security

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to resolve disputes, or to meet legal requirements. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, but reasonable measures should be used to protect information under the site operator's control.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Requests can be sent through the Contact page. We may need enough information to verify a request.

6. Children

KnowMint is not directed at children under the age at which online consent is required in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may change when the website, services or applicable requirements change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the website after a change means the updated policy applies to future visits.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the KnowMint contact page.
